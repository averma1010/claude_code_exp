I've been meaning to create a personal website for a while now, and I finally did it! This site is built with Next.js and deployed on Cloudflare Pages.

## Why I Built This

Having a personal corner of the internet is important. Social media platforms come and go, but your own website is yours forever. Here, I can write without algorithms, share what I want, and present myself the way I choose.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Blog**: My thoughts on technology, design, and whatever else interests me
- **Gallery**: Photos and videos from my life and travels
- **Currently**: What I'm reading, watching, and listening to right now

## The Tech Stack

I kept it simple:
- Next.js for the framework
- Tailwind CSS for styling
- MDX for blog posts
- Cloudflare Pages for hosting

Everything is simple, fast, and easy to maintain. No database, no complex build processes, just static files and Markdown.

## What's Next

I plan to write more regularly about projects I'm working on, interesting problems I solve, and things I learn. If you're interested, check back occasionally or subscribe to the RSS feed.

Thanks for visiting!
